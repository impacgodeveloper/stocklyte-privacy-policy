Request Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your StockLyte account and all associated personal data. Follow the steps below to submit your request.

How to submit a deletion request
  1. Send an email to our support team using the address below.
  2. Use the subject line Account Deletion Request and include the email address registered to your account.
  3. Our team will verify your identity and confirm the request within 3 business days.
  4. Your account and personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of confirmation.
Send your request to
info@impacgo.com
Include the email address linked to your StockLyte account in the message body.
Suggested subject line: Account Deletion Request — StockLyte
What data is deleted vs retained
Data Type Action Retention Period
Name, email address, phone number Deleted Within 30 days of request
Profile photo / avatar Deleted Within 30 days of request
Product images uploaded by account Deleted Within 30 days of request
Stock transaction records Retained Up to 90 days for business compliance, then deleted
Audit logs referencing your user ID Retained Up to 90 days for business compliance, then deleted
Aggregated / anonymised analytics Retained Indefinitely (no personal identifiers)
Processing timeline

After we receive your request:

  • Within 3 business days — identity verified and deletion confirmed via email.
  • Within 30 days — personal data permanently deleted from all systems.
  • Within 90 days — transaction and audit records purged after the compliance retention period.

IMPACGO SOLUTIONS PRIVATE LIMITED ("we," "our," or "us") operates the STOCKLYTE mobile application (the "App"), a professional inventory management platform for businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use STOCKLYTE on your Android or iOS device.

By downloading or using STOCKLYTE, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

01

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and data generated through your use of the App.

Category Data collected Purpose
Account Full name, email address, phone number, role, and authentication information processed through our authentication provider Authentication and team management
Profile Avatar photo, assigned location, and language preference Personalization and multi-location access
Inventory data Product names, SKUs, barcodes, descriptions, categories, pricing, stock levels, expiry dates, purchase records, and sales records Core inventory management functionality
Product images Photos captured or selected from your device Product identification and catalogue management
Stock movements Transaction type, quantities, timestamps, notes, and source/destination locations Audit trail and reporting
Team data Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and roles of team members added by authorized administrators User management within your organization
Device & technical Timezone and device type where required for application functionality and compatibility App functionality and device compatibility
Session information Authentication/session information stored on-device Maintaining an authenticated session
We do not collect GPS location data, contact lists, call logs, device identifiers such as IMEI/IDFA, or advertising identifiers. STOCKLYTE does not contain advertising.
02

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, secure, and improve STOCKLYTE, including to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions
  • Display and manage an organization's inventory, products, and stock levels
  • Process purchase and sales records
  • Generate reports, analytics dashboards, and CSV/PDF exports requested by users
  • Provide in-app alerts for low-stock thresholds and product expiry dates
  • Provide multi-user team management and role-based access controls
  • Maintain audit records of inventory transactions
  • Support available language options, including English, Hindi, and Telugu
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve application stability
  • Provide customer support
  • Protect the service against unauthorized access, fraud, misuse, or security threats

We do not use your business or inventory information for advertising or profiling unrelated to providing the STOCKLYTE service.

03

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may be shared or processed only in the following circumstances:

  • Supabase (Backend & Cloud Storage) — Supabase provides backend, authentication, database, and cloud-storage infrastructure used by STOCKLYTE, including storage of inventory data and product/avatar images. Supabase's privacy practices are described in its Privacy Policy.
  • Within your organization — Authorized administrators and team members may access business and inventory information according to the roles and permissions assigned within the organization.
  • Legal compliance — We may disclose information where required by applicable law, court order, legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of STOCKLYTE, our users, or others.
Third-party services: STOCKLYTE does not intentionally integrate advertising networks or third-party advertising SDKs. If additional third-party services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy and applicable store privacy disclosures may be updated accordingly.
04

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using infrastructure provided by Supabase and other infrastructure used to operate STOCKLYTE. All data transmitted between the App and our services is protected using HTTPS/TLS encryption.

Session information may be stored locally on your device to maintain an authenticated session. Authentication and session-related information may be transmitted to our backend services when required to authenticate users and operate the App.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our application services may be accessed through stocklyte.impacgo.com. Your information may be processed or stored in data centers operated by our service providers, which may be located outside your country of residence. Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to use service providers that maintain appropriate safeguards.

06

Data Retention

We retain account and business data for as long as the relevant organization or account remains active and as necessary to provide STOCKLYTE. If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period and within any timeframe required by applicable law, except where certain information must be retained for legal, security, accounting, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Audit records may be retained for up to 12 months where implemented for security, accountability, or legal purposes, after which they are permanently deleted.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Access — Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability — Request your information in a structured, machine-readable format where applicable
  • Objection or restriction — Object to or request restriction of certain processing where applicable
  • Withdrawal of consent — Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

If account creation is available, users can request deletion through Settings → Account → Delete Account in the App. Users may also submit an account deletion request by emailing info@impacgo.com. Deletion requests may be subject to identity verification.

To exercise other privacy rights, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section. We will review and respond to requests within a reasonable period and within any timeframe required by applicable law.

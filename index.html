Impacgo Technologies ("we," "our," or "us") operates the StockLyte mobile application (the "App"), a professional inventory management platform for businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use StockLyte on your Android or iOS device.

By downloading or using StockLyte, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

01

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and data generated through your use of the App.

Category Data collected Purpose
Account Full name, email address, phone number, role, password (hashed) Authentication and team management
Profile Avatar photo, assigned location, language preference Personalisation and multi-location access
Inventory data Product names, SKUs, barcodes, descriptions, categories, pricing, stock levels, expiry dates Core inventory management functionality
Product images Photos captured or selected from your device Product identification and catalogue
Stock movements Transaction type, quantities, timestamps, notes, source/destination locations Audit trail and reporting
Team data Names, emails, phone numbers, roles of team members added by admins User management within your organisation
Device & technical Timezone, device type (for UI rendering) Analytics display and app functionality
Session tokens Access token, refresh token (stored on-device) Keeping you securely signed in
We do not collect location data (GPS), contact lists, call logs, device identifiers (IMEI/IDFA), or any advertising identifiers. StockLyte does not contain ads.
02

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect exclusively to operate and improve StockLyte:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your session securely
  • Display and manage your organisation's inventory, products, and stock levels
  • Generate reports, analytics dashboards, and CSV/PDF exports you request
  • Send in-app alerts for low stock thresholds and product expiry dates
  • Provide multi-user team management with role-based access controls
  • Maintain an audit log of all inventory transactions for accountability
  • Support multi-language display (English, Hindi, Telugu)
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve App stability

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing the inventory management service.

03

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information is shared only in these limited circumstances:

  • Supabase (Backend & Cloud Storage) — Our backend infrastructure provider processes authentication, stores inventory data, and hosts product/avatar images. Supabase acts as a data processor under a data processing agreement. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Within your organisation — Admins and authorised team members can view inventory and team data within the same organisational account. Access is governed by the roles you assign.
  • Legal compliance — We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of users or the public.
StockLyte does not integrate with advertising networks, social media platforms, analytics SDKs (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics), or crash reporting services.
04

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase servers. All data transmission between the App and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Session tokens are stored in your device's secure local storage (SharedPreferences) and are never transmitted to third parties.

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data location: Our API is hosted at stocklyte.impacgo.com. Your data may be processed in data centres operated by Supabase, which may be located outside your country of residence.

05

App Permissions

StockLyte requests the following device permissions:

Permission Why it is needed
CAMERA Scanning product barcodes and capturing product photos
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Selecting existing images from your gallery for products and avatars
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Saving exported CSV and PDF reports to your device (Android 9 and below)
INTERNET Communicating with the StockLyte backend to sync inventory data
VIBRATE Haptic feedback during barcode scanning

All permissions are requested at runtime and can be revoked at any time through your device settings. Revoking a permission will disable the associated feature but will not affect your other App functionality.

06

Data Retention

We retain your account and inventory data for as long as your organisation's account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by applicable law or for legitimate business purposes (such as resolving disputes or complying with legal obligations).

Audit log entries may be retained for up to 12 months for compliance and accountability purposes, after which they are permanently deleted.

07

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Update inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — Object to processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent — Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

08

Children's Privacy

StockLyte is a professional business application intended for use by adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has registered, please contact us immediately.
